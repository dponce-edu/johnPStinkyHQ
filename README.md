# Club Escapade

I appreciate the desire to have some hard-earned fun after diligent classwork. I was that kid, myself, so forgive me this one indulgence in honor of my inner child. I *don't* appreciate not being in control of the *kind* of fun my students can have in class. After all, kid me just wanted to explore the internet-- adult me has a job to keep, and an inappropriate internet experience is the LAST thing I want to happen. So, my compromise is this: The boundless creativity found in an early internet arcade, but with a svelter shape and shelves that I personally stock. All links to other websites are disabled. All content is vetted. And, ultimately... all games are FUN.
